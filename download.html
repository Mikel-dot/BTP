Version 0.3

What's New?

  • + New GAME SITUATION section with comp archetype detection, pool picks that best fit your allied comp, recommended items and runes, power curves, and situational draft context.
  • + Direct import into the LoL client for items and runes from the draft, with optional auto-import on champion lock-in and page reuse to avoid profile clutter every match.
  • + New draft simulation mode to test picks, builds, and recommendations without having League of Legends open.
  • + Visual and layout improvements across the draft flow, item and rune tooltips, and the overall presentation of the assistant.
  • + Better Draft Assistant performance by delaying heavy calculations until the champion is locked in.
  • + Bug fixes for hover states, carousels, window sizing, and more consistent tool opening behavior.
