HOW TO USE BTP

From zero to expert in three steps. BTP is designed to be intuitive and silent. Set it up once and let it work in the background while you focus on winning.

The Command Center: From here you can launch the Draft Assistant, configure your Champion Pool, or review your Self-Coaching history.

Step 1: Configure your API Key

In order for the Self-Coaching module to download your actual stats after every match, we need to connect to Riot Games.

Go to Settings (the gear icon top right) or enter the Self-Coaching tab and click on "Riot API".

Enter your Riot ID (e.g., Faker#KR1), your region, and the Development API Key which you can generate for free on the Riot Developer Portal. Click Save and you're done!

You will only need to do this once. Your key will be encrypted locally for future sessions.

Step 2: Define your Arsenal

Click on Pool Builder from the main menu.

Here you will see tabs for each role (TOP, JGL, MID, ADC, SUP). Use the search bar to find your comfort champions and add them to your list. BTP will use this information to prioritize picks for you during the draft.

Pro Tip: Remember to click "Save Changes" before switching roles or closing the window.

Step 3: Play and Evaluate

Everything is set up! Minimize BTP and queue up for a game. If you have the option enabled in settings, the Draft Assistant will open automatically when a match is found.

When the Nexus explodes, BTP will detect the end of the game and launch a Mini-Popup on your screen. Take 10 seconds to rate your gameplay feel, vision, and farming. BTP will cross-reference your notes with the official stats and save them to your Self-Coaching history.

Advanced Statistical Analysis

To provide highly accurate recommendations during the Draft phase, BTP does not rely on raw win rates. We utilize a system of Deltas that isolates the true structural advantage of every matchup and shatters win rate myths.

Note: Below we will detail the mathematical calculation step-by-step using Counters (enemies) as an example. The calculation for Synergies (allies) is completely analogous, adding their strengths instead of subtracting them ($E[WR] = WR_A + WR_B - 0.5$).

In summary:

$\Delta_1$ (Raw Delta): Reflects the direct personal impact. Do you win or lose more than usual against this champion?

$\Delta_2$ (Normalized Delta): Reflects the true advantage of the ability kit. It isolates the meta to tell you if your champion is an authentic counter, ignoring whether the enemy is "broken" or nerfed.

1. Raw Delta ($\Delta_1$)

Delta 1 represents Personal Impact. It answers the question: How does this matchup alter my standard probability of winning?

The Delta 1 Trap: If you look at the example on the right, you will see that Darius loses a 3% win rate when playing against Teemo. Traditional websites will tell you: *"Teemo is a counter to Darius!"*.

But is this true? Does he counter him because Teemo's mechanics shut down Darius, or is Teemo simply statistically overpowered in this patch, beating everyone equally? Delta 1 is blind to this and can mislead us into banning the wrong champion.

The Formula

  • $WR_{real}$: The historical win rate of your champion specifically when facing this enemy.
  • $WR_{A}$: The global average win rate of your champion against the rest of the world.
$$ \Delta_1 = (WR_{real} - WR_{A}) \times 100 $$
The Mirage of a Counter:

You play Darius (Standard win rate $WR_{A} = 51\%$).
The data shows that against Teemo, Darius only wins $48\%$ of the time ($WR_{real}$).

$\Delta_1 = (0.48 - 0.51) \times 100 = \textbf{-3.00\%}$

Apparent Conclusion: Playing against Teemo penalizes you by 3%. It would seem like the ultimate counter, but the formula is ignoring Teemo's global state.

2. Normalized Delta ($\Delta_2$)

Here is where BTP's magic comes in. To know if Teemo is a *true counter* to Darius, we first calculate the Expected Win Rate ($E[WR]$). That is, we cross-reference the general form of both champions to find out what the math dictates "should" happen.

By subtracting the Expected Win Rate from the Real Win Rate, we obtain the True Advantage ($\Delta_2$), completely filtering out whether the enemy is overpowered or nerfed.

As you will see in the example on the right, a negative $\Delta_1$ can turn into an incredibly positive $\Delta_2$. This means your champion's kit naturally shuts down the enemy, even if they are "broken". This is the master metric that ranks the Tier List in your Draft.

Uncovering the Truth

  • $WR_{A}$: Your champion's global win rate.
  • $WR_{B}$: The enemy's global win rate on this patch.
  • $E[WR]$: The Expected Win Rate (the mathematical prediction).
$$ E[WR] = WR_{A} - WR_{B} + 0.5 $$
$$ \Delta_2 = (WR_{real} - E[WR]) \times 100 $$
The Resolution of the Teemo Case:

We discover that in this patch Teemo is incredibly overpowered ($WR_{B} = 58\%$).
Mathematically, your Darius ($WR_{A} = 51\%$) "should" win only $43\%$ of the matches against such a statistical anomaly ($E[WR] = 0.51 - 0.58 + 0.5 = 0.43$).

However, the data from the previous card told us that Darius actually wins $48\%$ ($WR_{real}$).

$\Delta_2 = (0.48 - 0.43) \times 100 = \textbf{+5.00\%}$

The Reality: Although Delta 1 was -3.00%, Delta 2 reveals a +5.00%. Teemo does not counter Darius; Teemo beats everyone, but Darius is one of the best answers in the game to stop him.
